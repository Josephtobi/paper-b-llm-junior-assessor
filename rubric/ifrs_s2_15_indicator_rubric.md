# 15-Indicator Weighted Ordinal Scoring Rubric (Max = 45)

Source: Davies (2026), *Ready for 2028? IFRS S1 and S2 Readiness of Nigeria's Listed and OTC Oil & Gas Public Interest Entities* (Zenodo), Appendix A.  
Used as the **human ground-truth instrument** for Paper B.

Scale (all indicators):

| Score | Label | Meaning |
| ---: | :--- | :--- |
| 0 | Omission | Complete absence of disclosure on the indicator |
| 1 | Boilerplate / Generic | High-level qualitative or template language without entity-specific quantitative substance |
| 2 | Partial / Entity-specific | Substantive entity-specific narrative or partial quantitative disclosure |
| 3 | Full investor-grade | Decision-useful quantitative/qualitative disclosure meeting the technical baseline |

## Pillar I — Governance (IFRS S2 paras. 5–7) — max 6

### GOV-1: Board Oversight
- **0:** No mention of board oversight of climate/sustainability risks.
- **1:** Generic corporate governance or risk oversight statement without climate specifics.
- **2:** Named Board Committee (e.g., Sustainability / RESG / HSSE) with stated mandate.
- **3:** Detailed charter, meeting frequency, competency tracking, and climate KPIs tied to executive remuneration.

### GOV-2: Management Role
- **0:** No mention of management-level sustainability roles.
- **1:** Vague reference to executive risk management without climate reporting lines.
- **2:** Specific executive role or department identified overseeing ESG/HSE matters.
- **3:** Dedicated cross-functional management committee, clear reporting lines to CEO/Board, and meeting cadence.

## Pillar II — Strategy (IFRS S2 paras. 8–23) — max 9

### STR-1: Climate Risks & Opportunities
- **0:** No identification of physical or transition climate risks.
- **1:** High-level macro acknowledgement without categorization.
- **2:** Categorized physical and transition risks with partial time horizons.
- **3:** Granular physical and transition risks mapped to assets/business lines across short/medium/long horizons.

### STR-2: Business Model & Value-Chain Impact
- **0:** No discussion of climate impact on business model or value chain.
- **1:** Generic CSR / environmental stewardship narrative.
- **2:** Qualitative operational adjustments or value-chain mapping.
- **3:** Detailed asset/facility vulnerability and value-chain strategic integration.

### STR-3: Climate Scenario Analysis
- **0:** No scenario analysis.
- **1:** Commitment to future analysis without data.
- **2:** Qualitative pathways without quantitative testing.
- **3:** Quantitative resilience testing against recognized scenarios (e.g., IEA CPS/STEPS/NZE) with asset/price sensitivity.

## Pillar III — Risk Management (IFRS S2 paras. 24–26) — max 6

### RM-1: ERM Integration
- **0:** No risk management framework disclosed.
- **1:** Generic ERM/HSE statement omitting climate.
- **2:** Standalone ESG/climate assessment or intermediate ERM linkage.
- **3:** Climate as a principal strategic risk in COSO/ISO 31000 ERM matrix.

### RM-2: Operational Mitigation & Adaptation
- **0:** No operational mitigation disclosed.
- **1:** Vague environmental protection statements.
- **2:** Specific mitigation projects (gas commercialization, flare-out, LDAR, etc.).
- **3:** Comprehensive programme linked to capex, EORF milestones, and AROs.

## Pillar IV — Metrics & Targets (IFRS S2 paras. 27–37; SASB) — max 9

### MET-1: Scope 1, 2 & 3 GHG Inventory
- **0:** No quantitative GHG disclosed.
- **1:** Partial (e.g., Scope 1 only) or narrative without Scope 2.
- **2:** Full Scope 1–2 with methodology.
- **3:** Disaggregated Scope 1–3 with intensity metrics and GHG Protocol baseline.

### MET-2: SASB Extractive Metrics (EM-EP/RM/SV)
- **0:** No SASB/extractive environmental metrics.
- **1:** Basic throughput/production/safety stats.
- **2:** Partial SASB metrics (flaring, spills, HSE).
- **3:** Full Volume 11 including reserve/asset carbon-price sensitivity.

### MET-3: Decarbonisation Targets
- **0:** No targets.
- **1:** Qualitative aspirations without numbers.
- **2:** Quantitative targets missing baseline/interim milestones.
- **3:** Baseline year, interim milestones, net-zero year, and scope coverage.

## Pillar V — NUPRC Alignment — max 6

### NUP-1: Methane MRV & Env. Tracking
- **0:** No NUPRC methane / env monitoring mention.
- **1:** Generic regulatory compliance claim.
- **2:** Acknowledgment with initial LDAR/OGI pilots or depot monitoring.
- **3:** Measurement-based methane MRV reconciled to NUPRC templates (OGMP 2.0 L4/5).

### NUP-2: Flaring Commercialisation & Spills
- **0:** No flaring/spill data.
- **1:** Basic qualitative HSSE / anti-flaring policy.
- **2:** Quantitative flare-out / gas monetization / spill tracking.
- **3:** Asset-level flare reduction volumes, zero-routine-flare milestones, penalty/baseline reconciliation.

## Pillar VI — Financial Connectivity — max 3

### FIN-1: IAS 36 & IAS 37 Connectivity
- **0:** Contradictory assumptions or complete omission of impairment/ARO notes.
- **1:** Standard IAS 36/37 notes with zero climate cross-reference.
- **2:** Explicit qualitative FS note that climate commitments were considered.
- **3:** Quantitative crosswalk of climate scenarios/carbon prices/flare-out into IAS 36/37.

## Pillar VII — Assurance Readiness — max 6

### ASR-1: Sustainability Boundary Alignment
- **0:** Organizational boundary undefined.
- **1:** General company/subsidiary mention without boundary rules.
- **2:** Clear operational-control boundary for operated assets.
- **3:** Dual-boundary (100% operated vs equity share) across JVs/PSCs.

### ASR-2: ICSR & ISSA 5000 Readiness
- **0:** No internal control/audit disclosure.
- **1:** ICFR / generic Internal Audit only.
- **2:** Internal Audit of ESG risks or planned partial external assurance.
- **3:** Formal COSO-aligned ICSR with external ISSA 5000 readiness.
